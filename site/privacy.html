What I collect

Only what you type into the free audit form: your name, email, website or profile link, what you'd like fixed, your time zone, the best time to reach you and any message you add.

To reply to you, run your free audit and, if you ask, talk about a fix. Nothing else. I don't sell, rent or share your details, and I don't add you to a mailing list.

Who handles it

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the form to my email and sends you the confirmation email.
  • Google hosts my email inbox, and serves the fonts on this site.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad trackers.

How long I keep it

As long as we're talking, or working together, and up to 12 months after. Ask and I'll delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you at any time. Email ahonsiclever@gmail.com and I'll do it within 30 days. This covers rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, the Australian Privacy Act and US state privacy laws.
