Services for creators

For creators, the problem is posting consistently. I fix that.

One recording becomes daily clips, cut, captioned and scheduled across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts and X, with a posting schedule and stats. It's the same system behind The Media Chic's weekly videos on the Work page.

Signs your posting needs a system

  • Gaps of 7 days or more between posts
  • Bursts of posts, then silence
  • Active on one platform, dead or missing on the others
  • Long videos and podcasts never cut into Shorts or Reels
  • Posting at random times
  • Views falling over your last 10–20 posts
  • "Sorry I've been gone" or "I'm back" posts
  • Livestreams never clipped
  • Same format every time, no captions or B-roll
  • Fans asking "when's the next video?"
  • A bio link still promoting an old launch
  • A big audience but no newsletter or community of your own
