Questions

Things people ask before starting.

What does the free audit include?

A check of your business against the 14 areas, using what anyone can see from outside: your site, reviews, socials, booking and event pages. You get the top problems, the proof for each and what fixing them involves. No obligation.

How fast will I hear back?

Within one working day. Fill in the form and you get an instant confirmation email; your audit follows within one working day.

Which tools do you build in?

Usually the ones you already pay for. I build in GoHighLevel, n8n, Zapier, HubSpot, Notion and ManyChat, plus custom code where it's cheaper or more reliable.

Do I need to change my CRM?

No. If your CRM is set up badly, I clean it up. I only suggest a new one if your current tool can't do the job.

Will AI send things to my customers without me knowing?

Only what you approve. AI messages pass a check before they send, and you can keep any step on "draft only" so you review it first.

Where are you, and when can we talk?

I work remotely with clients in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and I schedule calls in your time zone. Tell me your best time in the form.

Is there a contract?

Business fixes have a one-off setup fee, and the monthly plan is optional. Creator plans are monthly with no contract: cancel any time.

Who owns what you build?

You do. It lives in your accounts, and you get a written handover explaining how it works.
