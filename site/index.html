Conservatory Ballet · Virginia, USwith ABRB Consulting, LLCApr – Aug 2026

A full lead-to-client system for a ballet school

Enquiries were sitting in spreadsheets, website registrations never reached the CRM and emails landed in spam. I mapped the whole journey on paper, then built it end to end. The first build was live in four weeks, I was retained on contract, and in July alone I delivered 9 systems.

  • Ticket Sales Engine (n8n + Google Gemini). A CRM tag triggers personal AI-written emails for recitals, workshops and summer camps. A "traffic-light" check stops a blank or broken AI message from ever being sent, and every send is logged.
  • Lead intake in GoHighLevel. A branded form with separate consent for service and marketing texts, a 5-stage pipeline, and a first SMS sent only when the person agreed to texts.
  • Website to CRM. Website and Stripe registrations never reached the CRM. I replaced the site form so every registration lands in it.
  • Three pipelines, one per revenue stream. Trials, programs and camps, and events, with a 9-way interest split that puts every new lead in the right place.
  • Email deliverability. SPF, DKIM and DMARC on a separate sending subdomain, so emails reach the inbox without breaking the school's own mail.
  • Ads audit and phone line. Six fixes in the right order, a four-number scorecard (cost per enquiry, enquiry to trial, trial to enrolled, budget spent) and a verified toll-free texting number.

Services for creators

For creators, the problem is posting consistently. I fix that.

One recording becomes daily clips, cut, captioned and scheduled across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts and X, with a posting schedule and stats. It's the same system behind The Media Chic's weekly videos above.

Signs your posting needs a system

  • Gaps of 7 days or more between posts
  • Bursts of posts, then silence
  • Active on one platform, dead or missing on the others
  • Long videos and podcasts never cut into Shorts or Reels
  • Posting at random times
  • Views falling over your last 10–20 posts
  • "Sorry I've been gone" or "I'm back" posts
  • Livestreams never clipped
  • Same format every time, no captions or B-roll
  • Fans asking "when's the next video?"
  • A bio link still promoting an old launch
  • A big audience but no newsletter or community of your own

Questions

Things people ask before starting.

What does the free audit include?

A check of your business against the 14 areas, using what anyone can see from outside: your site, reviews, socials, booking and event pages. You get the top problems, the proof for each and what fixing them involves. No obligation.

How fast will I hear back?

Within one working day. Fill in the form and you get an instant confirmation email; your audit follows within one working day.

Which tools do you build in?

Usually the ones you already pay for. I build in GoHighLevel, n8n, Zapier, HubSpot, Notion and ManyChat, plus custom code where it's cheaper or more reliable.

Do I need to change my CRM?

No. If your CRM is set up badly, I clean it up. I only suggest a new one if your current tool can't do the job.

Will AI send things to my customers without me knowing?

Only what you approve. AI messages pass a check before they send, and you can keep any step on "draft only" so you review it first.

Where are you, and when can we talk?

I work remotely with clients in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and I schedule calls in your time zone. Tell me your best time in the form.

Is there a contract?

Business fixes have a one-off setup fee, and the monthly plan is optional. Creator plans are monthly with no contract: cancel any time.

Who owns what you build?

You do. It lives in your accounts, and you get a written handover explaining how it works.
