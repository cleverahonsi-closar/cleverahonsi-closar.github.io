Questions

Things people ask before starting.

What's the difference between the demo and a full plan?

The demo is one week of videos on the plan you choose, for ₦20,000. A full plan is a month of videos. If you continue after the demo, you just pay for the month.

Do I have to show my face or talk to the camera?

No. Many of the best formats use photos, clips of your work and text on screen. If you're happy on camera, we can use that too.

What do I need to send?

Phone clips of your work, photos (old ones are great), screenshots of results or kind messages, and a few stories from your career. We tell you exactly what's missing.

Which platforms is this for?

Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. They all use vertical video, so one video works on all three.

Who posts the videos?

You do, so your account stays in your hands. Each video comes with a posting note so it only takes a few minutes.

Can I change or reject a video?

Yes. Tell us what's wrong and we fix it. We also learn from it, so the same problem doesn't come back.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move up or down at the start of any month.

Is this AI?

We use software to research formats and speed up editing. The footage, the stories and the voice are yours, and every video is checked before you get it.
