Questions

What people ask before they start.

Something else on your mind? Email me and I'll answer it.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. No call, no card. You get the problems costing you most, the proof for each, and how I'd fix them. You only pay if you want me to build the fix.

What does a fix cost?

Most fixes are a one-off $400–1,500. You see a fixed price after the audit, before any work starts, so there are no surprises later.

Will I have monthly costs?

Only if a fix needs a paid tool, and you'll know that cost up front. A care plan from $150 a month is optional. It keeps things running and fixes anything that breaks.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I set everything up in your accounts, test it on real cases and give you a short written guide in plain English.

I don't use a CRM. Do I need one?

You don't need to buy anything first. If keeping your customers in one place would help, I set up a simple one for you.

Who actually does the work?

I do. Closar is run by me, Clever Ahonsi, so you deal with the person building your system from start to finish.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. It's built in your own accounts and nothing is locked to me. If we stop working together, it keeps running.

How long does it take?

You get a plan and timeline along with the fixed price. For Conservatory Ballet, the first system was live within four weeks.
